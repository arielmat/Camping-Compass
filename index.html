What is this?

The Adopt a Porta-Potty initiative: camps and crews claim a bank of porta-potties, decorate them with love, and become their stewards for the burn. This is where those builds live, get celebrated, and inspire the next one.

🎨

Decorate

Turn a plastic box into a disco, a jungle, a spa or a gallery. Bring joy to a 3am playa walk.

🧹

Care for it

Adoption means stewardship — keep it stocked, tidy and shining, and leave no trace when the burn ends.

⭐

Celebrate

Post photos, rate each other's builds, swap tips in the comments, and grow the tradition.
