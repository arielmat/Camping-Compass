Decoration & Care Guidelines

Adopting a potty is a privilege. These guidelines keep decorations safe, keep the sanitation crews able to do their job, and keep the playa pristine. When in doubt, ask first — contact us.

The golden rule: Never do anything that blocks servicing, damages the unit, endangers a user, or leaves anything behind. Decorations are temporary guests on equipment that belongs to a vendor and must be pumped daily.

✅ Encouraged

  • Decorate the outside walls, door and roof-line with removable art — vinyl, fabric, banners, string lights.
  • Use battery / solar LED lighting and low-voltage decor that you take home.
  • Add tasteful signage, welcome messages, themed lettering and playa humor.
  • Keep it stocked: check for TP, hand sanitizer and working latches (report shortages to the vendor / DPW — don't restock the tank yourself).
  • Keep it clean & MOOP-free: sweep the surrounds, pick up litter, wipe handles.
  • Attach decorations with removable methods — zip ties, tension frames, magnets, low-tack tape, gaffer tape.
  • Photograph your build and share it in the gallery to inspire others.
  • Fully de-decorate before exodus and pack out every scrap.

⛔ Not permitted

  • Blocking or obstructing service — nothing over the pump port, vent stack, roof, or that stops the door swinging freely.
  • Permanent modification: no drilling, screwing, gluing, painting or writing directly on the unit.
  • Anything inside the tank or that could fall into it (no glitter bombs, confetti, loose beads, or objects near the seat).
  • Open flame, candles, incense or anything hot near a plastic, ventilated unit.
  • Mains power / generators / exposed wiring or trip hazards across the doorway.
  • Locks, latches or barriers that could trap someone inside, or anything that impedes accessibility (keep ADA units clear).
  • Explicit, hateful, or harassing imagery — this is a shared space for everyone.
  • Adopting a potty you didn't — don't alter or remove another crew's decorations without asking.
  • Leaving anything behind. Zero MOOP. If you brought it, it goes home with you.

The principles behind the rules

1

Service comes first

Every unit is pumped daily by the vendor. If a decoration slows, blocks, or endangers that crew, it comes down — no exceptions. Leave the pump port, vents and door path completely clear.

2

Leave No Trace

Matter Out Of Place (MOOP) is the enemy. Every zip tie, sequin and cable must leave with you. Decorate with pack-out in mind from day one.

3

Safety & access for all

No flame, no mains power, no trip hazards, no way to trap someone inside. Keep accessible units usable for the people who need them.

4

It's not yours to keep

The units are rented equipment. Never modify them permanently, and never mess with a build another crew has adopted.

5

Radical inclusion

Everyone uses these, day and night. Keep art welcoming — funny and beautiful, never explicit, hateful, or exclusionary.

These community guidelines complement — they do not replace — Burning Man's official rules, your vendor's terms, and BLM regulations. Always follow the current official guidance. Got a question about whether something's allowed? Ask us before you build.
